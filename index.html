Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade from an HDB to a condo if I still have a home loan?

Yes, you can, but it depends on whether you choose to sell first or buy first. Your loan options will also shift from MSR (for HDB) to TDSR (for condo). I will calculate your exact borrowing capacity based on your income, outstanding HDB loan, CPF, and credit factors.

Should I buy a condo first or sell my HDB first?

Both choices are possible.

  • Sell first: safer financially and usually means no ABSD, but requires careful timeline planning.
  • Buy first: more convenient, but requires meeting TDSR rules and having enough cash and CPF.

I help you evaluate which approach is safest for your situation.

Do I need to pay ABSD when upgrading from HDB to condo?

Not if you sell your HDB before you exercise the condo OTP.

If you buy first, a married couple with at least one Singapore Citizen buying jointly pays ABSD upfront and can get it refunded by selling the HDB within 6 months of buying a completed condo, or within 6 months of TOP for a new launch. For a new EC bought from the developer, eligible married couples get the ABSD remitted upfront.

With proper planning, most HDB upgraders do not end up paying ABSD. I will map out your exact timeline.

How much cash and CPF do I need to upgrade to a condo?

This depends on:

  • Your HDB sale proceeds
  • Outstanding loan
  • Condo budget
  • Loan eligibility
  • CPF used for your HDB

Most upgraders use a combination of HDB sale proceeds and CPF refund to fund their condo downpayment. I provide a full affordability breakdown during the upgrade assessment.

Is upgrading to a condo better than buying another HDB?

It depends on your goals. Condos provide facilities, privacy, and long-term capital growth potential. HDBs offer affordability and larger space in some cases. I provide a customised comparison based on your finances, lifestyle, and future plans.

Should I choose an Executive Condo (EC) or a private condo?

ECs are usually more affordable, come with grants for some buyers, and have historically appreciated well after their 5-year MOP, though that is not guaranteed. Condos offer more flexibility, more locations, and no MOP. Your choice depends on budget, timing, and lifestyle needs, and I'll walk you through both options.

How long does it take to upgrade from HDB to condo?

Most families complete the full journey in about 4 to 8 months when buying a completed home, depending on your plan and how quickly the HDB sells. A detailed timeline will help you avoid renting or rushing.

What is the main mistake HDB owners make when upgrading?

Buying without a complete financial plan, especially without knowing exact cashflow, CPF refunds, and loan eligibility. This often leads to affordability shocks, renting, or forced sales. My process is built to prevent this.

What are the minimum eligibility rules to buy a condo after selling HDB?

You must:

  • Be at least 21
  • Have stable income
  • Pass the bank loan assessment (TDSR)

There is no citizenship restriction for buying private condos, although ABSD rates differ for Singapore Citizens, PRs and foreigners. ECs have their own HDB eligibility rules, including an income ceiling.
