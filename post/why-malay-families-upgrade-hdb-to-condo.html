Why More Malay Families Are Choosing to Upgrade from HDB to Condo

By Mirta Syazanna · 3 min read · Last reviewed November 2026

For years, many Malay families believed that upgrading from an HDB to a condo was only for the wealthy. Today, more and more Malay households are making the move from HDB to a condo or Executive Condo (EC). Here’s why.

1. Executive Condos (ECs) are the natural first step

ECs are the “sweet spot” for HDB upgraders. They start at lower prices than private condos, yet still offer full facilities and room for capital appreciation. For many Malay families, an EC is the first step into private housing.

2. Selling the HDB at the right time

HDB resale prices move in cycles. Many Malay homeowners choose to sell while their flat’s value is strong and use the gains to upgrade, rather than wait and risk their flat’s value levelling off as the lease runs down. See the latest HDB resale prices by town.

3. Building long-term wealth

A condo or EC isn’t just about lifestyle; it’s about capital appreciation. Over 10–15 years, private homes have historically grown faster in value than HDB flats, helping families build a stronger financial legacy.

4. Cultural and family considerations

Many Malay families want larger spaces for multi-generational living, halal-friendly kitchens, and room for hosting family and friends. Condos often provide space and amenities that suit these needs better.

5. Planning the loan properly

Interest rates rise and fall. Families who plan their repayments with a buffer for higher rates see upgrading as a measured step rather than a gamble.

6. Lifestyle aspirations

Access to facilities like pools, gyms, and secure environments appeals to younger Malay families. Beyond lifestyle, it’s also about giving children more space and opportunities.

7. Cooling measures reward planning

Cooling measures and loan limits have pushed families to be more intentional with their moves. Malay homeowners are planning their upgrades earlier, so they don’t miss their window of affordability.

The HDB-to-condo upgrade isn’t just a dream for Malay families in Singapore. With the right planning, you can build both lifestyle and legacy for your family.
