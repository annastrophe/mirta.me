HDB resale prices in the North, North-East and East

Every HDB resale registered in the North, North-East and East of Singapore from Dec 2025 to Nov 2026, compared with the 12 months before. Updated automatically every week from HDB data on data.gov.sg. Last updated 2026-11-25.

  • North-East (Punggol, Sengkang, Hougang, Serangoon, Ang Mo Kio), Dec 2025 to Nov 2026: 4-room median $650,000 from 2,770 sales (down 0.8% on the 12 months before); 5-room median $760,000 from 1,594 sales (up 2.0% on the 12 months before).
  • North (Woodlands, Yishun, Sembawang), Dec 2025 to Nov 2026: 4-room median $555,000 from 2,046 sales (down 3.5% on the 12 months before); 5-room median $660,000 from 1,065 sales (down 2.9% on the 12 months before).
  • East (Tampines, Pasir Ris, Bedok), Dec 2025 to Nov 2026: 4-room median $650,000 from 1,783 sales (about the same as the 12 months before); 5-room median $780,000 from 1,105 sales (up 0.6% on the 12 months before).
  • The highest HDB resale price in these towns over the period was $1,588,000, for Blk 152B Bedok South Road in Bedok (Nov 2026).
